    How to play

    Clear the board by pairing every country with the clue that belongs to it.

    • 1Every country has one partner tile on the board: its capital, its flag, its currency or its population. Tap the two to clear them.
    • 2A tile is free when nothing sits on top of it and its left or right side is open. Shaded tiles are blocked until you uncover them.
    • 3One puzzle a day, the same board for everyone. The clock runs while you play and pauses when you leave the tab.
    • 4Hints and shuffles are always there, but they show up in your result grid.
