How to play

Tap two free tiles that belong to the same country. A tile is free when nothing sits on top of it and its left or right side is open. Shaded tiles are blocked.

Capital and administrative-seat notes

Conventional capitals are used for ordinary regions. Where no single capital city exists, the verified administrative seat is used and named below. Entries without a usable current seat stay flag-only.

  • American Samoa: Pago Pago is the conventional capital label; government functions are in neighbouring Fagatogo and Utulei.
  • Hong Kong: Tamar is the administrative centre, not a separate capital city.
  • Macau: São Lourenço is the location of Macau Government Headquarters, used as its administrative seat.
  • French Southern and Antarctic Lands: Saint-Pierre, Réunion is the administration headquarters outside the territory.
  • Bougainville: Buka is the current interim capital; Arawa is the former capital.
  • Montserrat: Brades is the current de facto seat; Plymouth is the former capital.
  • British Indian Ocean Territory: London is the official remote administration seat; local government operates from Diego Garcia.
  • Bonaire: Kralendijk is the municipal seat.
  • Saba: The Bottom is the municipal seat.
  • Sint Eustatius: Oranjestad is the municipal seat.
  • Abkhazia: Sukhumi is the de facto capital of the disputed-status administration.
  • South Ossetia: Tskhinvali is the de facto capital of the disputed-status administration.
  • Transnistria: Tiraspol is the de facto capital of the disputed-status administration.
  • Northern Cyprus (TRNC): North Nicosia is the seat of the disputed-status administration; Cyprus also uses Nicosia.
  • Western Sahara (Sahrawi Republic): Tifariti is the temporary de facto capital of the SADR; Laayoune is the claimed capital and some offices are in exile.
  • Flanders: Brussels is the Flemish government seat, outside the Flemish Region.
  • Canary Islands: The Canary Islands have two capitals, so the clue names both.
  • Castile and León: Valladolid is the de facto government seat; no formal capital is designated.
  • Basque Country: Vitoria-Gasteiz is the de facto government seat.
  • Trentino-South Tyrol: Trento is the regional capital; the regional assembly alternates between Trento and Bolzano.
  • Tokyo: Tokyo is the conventional prefectural-capital label; the metropolitan government offices are in Shinjuku.
  • Antarctica: Antarctica has no single government or capital.
  • Artsakh (historical flag): Historical Artsakh flag only; no current capital for this historical entity.
  • Tokelau: Tokelau has no official capital; its government meeting location rotates among the three atolls.
  • Sark: Sark has a traditional capital and dispersed government offices, not a verified current capital city for this quiz.

          How to play

          Clear the board by pairing every country with the clue that belongs to it.

          • 1Every country has one partner tile on the board: its capital, its flag, its currency or its population. Tap the two to clear them.
          • 2A tile is free when nothing sits on top of it and its left or right side is open. Shaded tiles are blocked until you uncover them.
          • 3One puzzle a day, the same board for everyone. The clock runs while you play and pauses when you leave the tab. Rather not race? Switch to Untimed up top: you still get your streak and your grid, just no time.
          • 4Hints and shuffles are always there, but they show up in your result grid.
